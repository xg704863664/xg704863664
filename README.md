# Hi — I build small, practical things

I make free and low-cost resources for people who grow their own food, keep a few animals, and prefer knowing how things work.

## What I maintain

**[Herbal Home Remedies & Small-Space Growing](https://xg704863664.github.io/herbal-home-remedies/)** — a set of practical guides:

- [Herbal remedies for coughs](https://xg704863664.github.io/herbal-home-remedies/herbal-remedies-for-coughs.html) and [for sleep](https://xg704863664.github.io/herbal-home-remedies/herbal-remedies-for-sleep.html)
- [How to dry herbs at home](https://xg704863664.github.io/herbal-home-remedies/how-to-dry-herbs.html) — temperatures and times per herb
- [Backyard chickens for beginners](https://xg704863664.github.io/herbal-home-remedies/backyard-chickens-for-beginners.html) and [coop sizing](https://xg704863664.github.io/herbal-home-remedies/chickens.html)
- [Starting a vegetable garden](https://xg704863664.github.io/herbal-home-remedies/five-minute-garden.html) and [raised bed soil mix](https://xg704863664.github.io/herbal-home-remedies/raised-bed-soil-mix.html)
- [Food storage chart](https://xg704863664.github.io/herbal-home-remedies/food-storage-chart.html) — how long staples actually keep

The writing rule across all of it: give the amount and the temperature, include the caveats, and label traditional use as traditional use rather than implying it is proven.
